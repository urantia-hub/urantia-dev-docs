The API serves **The Upanishads, Part 1** (F. Max Muller, 1879) as JSON, one passage at a time, by the reference people use. Teachings of the Vedas on the self and Brahman. It has 207 passages in 3 upanishads. No key and no account.

## Get a passage

```bash
# Khandogya 6.8
curl https://api.urantia.dev/scriptures/upanishads/Khandogya%206.8
```

```json
      }

}
```

The slug `upanishads`, the id `upanishads-muller-1879`, and the prefix `upanishad` all name this text.

## References

`Khandogya 6.8` (prapathaka and khanda; each passage is one whole khanda, with its verse numbers in the text), `Kena 1.3` (khanda and verse), and `Isa 8` (verse).

`GET /scriptures/upanishads` lists every upanishad with its first and last reference.

## Search

```bash
# By meaning
curl -X POST https://api.urantia.dev/scriptures/search/semantic \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"q":"your question","corpus":"upanishads","limit":5}'
# By keyword
curl 'https://api.urantia.dev/works/search?q=your%20words&work=upanishads'
```

Each passage also links to the closest paragraphs of the Urantia Papers: `GET /scriptures/upanishads/Khandogya%206.8/urantia-parallels`. The [world religions guide](/scriptures) explains how the links are made, and the [MCP server](/mcp-servers) has the same calls for AI agents.

## Source

- **Text:** The Upanishads, Part 1, translated by F. Max Muller (1879)
- **Source:** [en.wikisource.org](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Sacred_Books_of_the_East/Volume_1)
- **License:** Public domain in the United States
- **Notes:** The three Upanishads of Sacred Books of the East 1 that are on Wikisource: the Khândogya, by book and Khanda (Khandogya 6.8, one passage for each Khanda, with Müller's verse numbers); the Kena or Talavakâra, by Khanda and verse (Kena 1.3); and the Îsâ, by verse (Isa 6). The Aitareya and the Kaushîtaki of that volume are not on Wikisource, so they are not here.
